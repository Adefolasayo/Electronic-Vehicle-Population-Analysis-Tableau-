# Electric Vehicle Population Analysis Dashboard (Tableau)

## Overview
This project presents an interactive dashboard analyzing the population of electric vehicles (EVs).  
The analysis provides insights into EV adoption trends, distribution across regions, and category-specific growth patterns.

The live dashboard can be accessed here:

## Tools Used
- Tableau (Data Visualization & Dashboard Design)

---

## Key Analytical Questions

### 1. EV Population Trends
- How has the total number of electric vehicles changed over time?
- Are adoption rates increasing year-over-year?

### 2. Regional Distribution
- Which regions have the highest EV populations?
- Geographic concentration and trends.

### 3. Vehicle Category Insights
- What types of electric vehicles (cars, bikes, etc.) are most common?
- Trends in category-specific adoption.

### 4. Market Growth Patterns
- Identification of rapid-growth regions and vehicle segments.
- Comparison across different years and categories.

---

## Insights Generated
- Identified regions leading in EV adoption.
- Trends highlighting which EV categories are most popular.
- Year-over-year growth patterns to support policy and business decisions.
- Data-driven insights to inform infrastructure planning and marketing strategies.

---

## Project Outcome
This dashboard enables stakeholders to:
- Monitor EV adoption trends across regions and vehicle categories.
- Make data-driven decisions for policy, marketing, or investment.
- Track market growth and identify high-potential segments.
